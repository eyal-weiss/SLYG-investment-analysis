- **So what (growth duration — the load-bearing read):**
  - **The deceleration is real but small, and the seasonal shape argues against panic.** Shelly's revenue is heavily H2-weighted (Black Friday). H1 growth has run well below FY growth in each of the last two years: H1'25 +29% → FY25 +40%. H1'26 at +26.7% is only ~2.6pp below the equivalent H1'25 rate. This is a *gentle* fade, which is what a long-duration model wants to see.
  - **But the level now sits materially below our base case's starting point.** Our model fades from a ~45% historical CAGR. The company has printed +26% (Q1) and +27% (Q2) — 2026 is tracking closer to +27–30% than +40%. If the front phase of the 3-phase fade starts ~10pp lower than modelled, the base EUR 92.38 compresses even with the 15-year window intact. **This is the number to re-derive after 12 Aug, not now.**
  - **Required H2 acceleration (my derivation, on the like-for-like base above):** to reach EUR 195–205M, H2 2026 must be EUR 126.7–136.7M = **+32.3% to +42.7% YoY**, versus H2 2025's actual +47%. So guidance itself embeds a deceleration and is arithmetically reachable — but it needs H2 growth roughly 6–16pp above the H1 run-rate.
  - **Caveat on the derivation:** all prelim ad-hocs report "revenue from devices and related services", and the FY2025 prelim used the same wording for the EUR 149.7M figure, so the series is like-for-like. It may still differ slightly from the audited consolidated revenue line the guidance is formally set against. Treat the +32–43% as accurate to ~1pp, not exact.

### 2. Subtle softening in the internal-expectations language — 13 Jul 2026
- **Fact:** Q1 2026 (14 Apr): "Revenue performance in the first quarter was **above** the Company's internal expectations." H1 2026 (13 Jul): "continued **in line with** the Company's **original** internal expectations."
- **Source:** the two ad-hocs linked above.
- **So what:** A step down from "above" to "in line", plus the unusual qualifier "original", is a soft negative — it reads as Q2 having run slightly behind Q1's beat. Low-weight signal on its own; worth checking against the 12 Aug commentary.
- **Correction to my own earlier read — stated plainly:** I initially flagged that the ad-hoc failed to reaffirm the EUR 195–205M / EUR 47–52M FY guidance as a warning sign. **That flag was wrong and I am withdrawing it.** Checking precedent: the H1 2025 ad-hoc (8 Jul 2025) likewise made no FY guidance statement, and the Q1 2026 ad-hoc made none either. The company reaffirms guidance in its *full-year* prelim (the Jan-2026 release explicitly said "The revenue target of c. EUR 200 million for 2026 remains unchanged") but not in its half-year prelims. The July-2026 omission **matches its own established practice** and carries no signal.

### 3. Cash position: working-capital drag reversing — 13 Jul 2026
- **Fact:** Cash and equivalents +~62% in Q2 2026 vs Q1 and +~72% YoY, "primarily reflecting continued positive developments in operating cash flow"; the company "has already reached a cash position that is close to the lower end of its year-end guidance of EUR 35 million to EUR 45 million." Q1 had already flagged cash +45% vs year-end 2025 to EUR 19.9M, with trade receivables "expected to normalize over the remainder of the year."
- **So what:** **The clearest positive in the window, and it hits our single biggest cited risk.** The thesis flags working-capital absorption (FY2025 FCF only EUR 1.5M on 138-day DSO) as the reason FCF lags EBIT. Two consecutive quarters of receivable normalisation plus reaching the year-end cash target at mid-year is the first hard evidence of the reversal. If it holds on 12 Aug, near-term FCF conversion in the model is conservative — which is directionally supportive of duration, since self-funded growth needs no dilution.
